The owner selected the first seven positions from the Release 2.0 marketing
review, then approved a Cycle day editor as position eight and immersive Care
as position nine. The nine titled 6.9-inch images under
`artifacts/app-store/iphone-69/candidate-v9/final/` were uploaded to ASC on
2026-10-03 in this order:

This order leads with acute Care, then period context and the benefit of a
populated Comfort Kit. Privacy, paid Patterns depth, and a bounded clinician
report follow. The Cycle day editor shows flow and a saved symptom without a
second Today screenshot. The two paid Patterns frames carry a small `With Plus`
badge. The source app views were captured through complete app navigation with
fictional local records; the QA harness is not storefront evidence.

The Privacy frame is text-heavy. The native report frame has awkward category
wrapping and the next card begins at the bottom edge. The owner accepted the
uploaded set with those visible limitations. See the candidate-v9 README and
manifest for image provenance, exact routes, dimensions, and hashes.

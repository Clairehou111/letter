# Product contract

Status: Release 2.0 target. Letter Within 1.0 Build 17 was submitted for iPhone
review on 2026-10-03; 2.0 release gates remain open.

Letter Within is a private period and cycle companion for people navigating
PMS, PMDD, or difficult cycle days. It combines explicit tracking with
supportive Care, personal memory, grounded Patterns, and records a person can
choose to share with a clinician. It is not a diagnostic tool, treatment,
emergency service, or substitute for professional care.

## The product loop

1. **Notice:** record dates, symptoms, mood, impact, notes, or a need.
2. **Care:** choose an escapable, low-effort experience during a hard moment.
3. **Remember:** optionally record Better, Same, or Worse and keep a useful
   action or the person's own words.
4. **Prepare:** return selected Comfort Kit items and a qualified, estimated
   Comfort Window when evidence supports it.
5. **Understand:** inspect source-linked Patterns and decide whether to export
   a factual record for a clinician.

The person authors the meaning of their experience. Letter Within may show
observations and transparent hypotheses; it does not infer causes, diagnosis,
severity, treatment effects, or what a relationship or decision “really means.”
The natural unit of use is a cycle, not a daily streak. Missing days are
acknowledged rather than punished or filled in.

## Release boundaries

- Health history is encrypted on the device. Account, entitlement, and optional
  consented operational services are separate; public copy must not imply that
  no app data ever leaves the device.
- No AI model reads, rewrites, summarizes, recommends from, or interprets
  health data. Predictions, matching, Patterns, and reports are deterministic
  local computation.
- Acute Care, safety routes, core recording, corrections, deletion, and local
  backup remain available without Plus. [Entitlements](entitlements.md)
  defines the paid boundary.
- Do not present fertility, ovulation, phase, PMDD, clinical severity, or crisis
  predictions. Do not promise symptom relief or treatment.
- Use inclusive, plain language without assuming gender, pregnancy intent,
  regular cycles, or clinician access. Safety and medical handoffs remain
  direct and visible.

The first public distribution is an iPhone app for the United States and
Canada. Android is an engineering target, not a current public availability
claim. Web is a memory-only development preview.

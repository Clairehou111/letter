# Experience contract

Status: Release 2.0 target. Detailed acceptance lives in
[2.0 requirements](features/release-2.0/requirements.md) and
[Care relief](features/release-2.0/care-relief.md).

## Navigation and language

The four primary destinations are Today, Cycle, Care, and Patterns. Settings is
reachable within two steps from a daylight screen, but absent from an active
immersive Care scene. Today is a useful entry point, not a daily attendance
task. Cycle shows user-recorded dates and qualified estimates. Care offers an
escapable action first. Patterns show evidence and missingness without implying
diagnosis or certainty.

Use calm, direct, inclusive language. Do not assume gender, pregnancy intent,
regular cycles, or access to a clinician. The person can skip, correct, delete,
or leave; there are no streaks or punitive empty states. Keep safety access
visible during Care, and never hide exit behind a timed interaction.
The retired App Lock control and cloud-AI preference do not return. Settings
explains the local health-data boundary and offers the app-switcher Screen Cover
and separate optional analytics control.

## Care

The five established Care entrances are Explode, Heavy, Racing, Space, and
Physical. Preserve their exit, safety, completion, and persistence boundaries.
The 2.0 body-care and Everyday care exceptions are specified in
[Care relief](features/release-2.0/care-relief.md): optional self-paced practices,
anatomically legible guidance, a companion cat that respects Reduced Motion,
and qualified review of SP6/LV3 locator art and copy before release. Care
gestures do not create a symptom rating or clinical conclusion. The optional
Better/Same/Worse response is a user-authored local record.

The Comfort Kit draws only from explicitly pinned or kept items, Better Care
outcomes, and user-authored future-self notes. It remains available in Care
once formed. A projected Comfort Window may surface the Kit only when its
evidence and reliability gates pass; neither is shown as a countdown to a
breakdown. Reminder notifications require separate explicit opt-in.

## Visual and accessibility rules

2.0 uses the Quiet Dusk experience system: warm, low-luminance plum app chrome,
tonal hierarchy, a restrained coral action accent, and still, dim headache
states. In-app letters may sit on inset warm paper; clinician PDFs use neutral
white print styling. Avoid flashing, strong pulses, noisy texture, harsh
contrast, and decorative motion that competes with instructions. Do not rely on
color or motion alone. Support safe areas, minimum 44 logical-pixel targets,
screen-reader descriptions, Dynamic Type, Increase Contrast, and Reduced Motion.
The release validation gate checks these on supported devices.

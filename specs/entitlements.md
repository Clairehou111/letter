# Access and entitlements

Status: Release 2.0 target. Store products, localized prices, and purchase
behavior require release validation.

| Access | Included |
| --- | --- |
| Free | Period estimates, local record review and correction, all acute Care and safety routes, encrypted local backup, basic Comfort Kit, factual programme records, manual BBT and factual Care memory; report previews, raw source CSV for any range including All records, and a factual Visit Summary PDF covering the recent three calendar months. |
| Plus | Personal Patterns and deeper cross-cycle explanation; factual Visit Summary PDFs for longer or custom ranges, and Clinical Pattern Report PDF. |

Reference prices are **$7.99 monthly**, **$39.99 yearly**, and **$99.99
lifetime**. The App Store's localized product metadata is authoritative in the
app and public purchase UI. Restore purchases and entitlement reconciliation
must work with the authenticated operational account.

A no-card Plus preview begins only when Plus-grade evidence first exists,
covers the next cycle, and lasts no more than 45 days. It can demonstrate
in-app interpretation, but does not grant paid PDF generation. The free CSV
and recent factual Visit Summary PDF remain available during or after a
preview. Only the file action checks PDF entitlement; choosing and reviewing
any range is free. Sensitive categories default off, and notes/reflections
also require individual source selection. A paywall must describe this
distinction before purchase. Acute Care, core records,
safety, correction, deletion, and backup never depend on Plus. Losing access
must not delete local history or previously generated files. Offline access
uses a still-valid locally known entitlement until reconciliation.

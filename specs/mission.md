# Letter Mission

Letter is a private period tracker that helps people understand what their body
communicates across each cycle.

The product must:

- track and predict cycles without treating pregnancy as the default goal
- make everyday symptom recording fast through taps, text, or voice
- help users prepare before difficult PMS/PMDD days
- provide low-effort emotional and physical support during symptom episodes
- learn which coping actions helped the individual
- turn accumulated records into useful patterns and doctor-ready evidence
- keep readable health history on the user's device by default

Product promise:

> Your body writes a letter every month. We help you read between the lines to
> understand your PMS, energy, and flow.

# Letter Mission

Letter is a private period tracker that helps people get through difficult
cycle-related moments and carry their own knowledge forward.

The product must:

- track and predict cycles without treating pregnancy as the default goal
- make symptoms, thoughts, and support actions easy to record
- reduce decisions and stimulation during difficult emotional or physical moments
- help the user delay impulsive actions without taking ownership away from them
- ask for reflection only later, when the user chooses and has more capacity
- remember the user's own words, comfort actions, boundaries, and reported outcomes
- return relevant personal guidance during a similar future moment
- organize patterns and doctor-ready evidence without diagnosing or inventing causes
- keep readable health history on the user's device by default

Product promise:

> Your body writes in patterns. Letter helps you through hard moments,
> remembers what helped, and brings your own wisdom back when you need it.

The user authors the meaning. Letter can preserve observations and offer
hypotheses, but it must never claim to reveal an objective hidden truth about a
relationship, job, body, or mind.

See `product-philosophy.md` for the complete product and safety boundaries.

# Privacy-Safe Operational Analytics Validation

Status: proposed

- An allowlist rejects cycle, symptom, Care, text, report, and inferred-state
  fields.
- No free-form analytics property is accepted.
- Opt-out prevents collection and does not impair local features.
- Retention and deletion behavior are documented and tested.
- Ordinary analytics contains no distress-state or clinical usefulness claim.
- Safety studies use a separate consented protocol and data store.

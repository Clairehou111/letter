# Deterministic Candidate Confirmation Validation

Status: historical validation plan superseded by the permanent no-AI product constraint

The former cloud-payload criterion belonged to the optional-LLM proposal. It is
retired and was a proposed acceptance criterion, not evidence that health data
was sent to or processed by a cloud model. Preserve historical validation
results as recorded; future validation uses the deterministic, on-device
criteria below.

- Deterministic on-device rule matching identifies supported candidate phrases
  without creating a saved rating.
- Negated symptoms are not presented as present without confirmation.
- Every candidate shows evidence and can be edited or rejected.
- Candidate matching works without network access and sends no health content to
  AI services.
- No output assigns severity, diagnosis, safety, treatment effect, or medication
  guidance.
- Sealed drafts never enter processing without explicit excerpt selection.

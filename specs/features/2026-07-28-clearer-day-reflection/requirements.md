# Clearer-Day Reflection And Future-Self Note Requirements

Status: validated
Branch: `feature/phase2-personal-care-memory`
Approved together with the remaining Phase 2 scope on 2026-07-28.

## Goal

On a user-chosen clearer day, turn one recorded Care moment into an editable
reflection and a short note the user may choose to see in a similar future
moment.

## Requirements

REQ-001: Reflection is available only for a persisted Care record and begins
only after the user opens it. Do not infer that today is psychologically or
hormonally clear and do not notify the user.

REQ-002: Ask at most three optional questions: `Does any part of this still
feel true?`, `What did you need, if you know?`, and `What helped, even a
little?`

REQ-003: Need choices are `Boundaries`, `Connection`, `Autonomy`,
`Rest or physical capacity`, `Something else`, and `Not sure`.

REQ-004: Free-text answers and the future-self note are optional, user-authored,
limited to 280 characters each, and have autocorrect and suggestions disabled.

REQ-005: The review screen clearly separates `What happened`, `What I needed`,
`What helped`, and `Next time`. Nothing is interpreted or generated by Letter.

REQ-006: Saving requires explicit confirmation. The user can edit or permanently
delete the reflection and future-self note later.

REQ-007: A future-self note is surfaced only for the matching Care mode and
only after the first acute interaction has already responded. It never blocks
Care, autoplays, or replaces safety guidance.

REQ-008: Dismissal does not mark a note helpful. Letter does not claim the note
is medically timed, subconscious truth, or evidence that PMS caused an event.

REQ-009: Reflection and note data remain in the encrypted local health store on
native devices and memory-only on Web. No cloud, analytics, API, or LLM use.

REQ-010: Back, discard, and repository-failure paths must not silently save
partial text. Unsaved text is cleared when the reflection flow is left.

REQ-011: Support Reduced Motion, 320px at 200 percent text, screen readers, and
44px targets.

## Non-Goals

- AI interpretation, NLP extraction, diagnosis, or clinical scoring
- automatic cycle-phase prompts or notifications
- relationship, employment, or medical advice
- preserving acute private drafts inside reflections

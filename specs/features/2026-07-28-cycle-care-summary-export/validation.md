# Cycle And Care Summary Export Validation

Status: proposed

- Every displayed value traces to a local user-confirmed record or factual Care
  event.
- Provenance and missingness are visible without opening a narrative detail.
- Unresolved candidates and private drafts are excluded.
- PDF and CSV match the preview and update after source deletion.
- No report says that PMS/PMDD or a treatment caused an outcome.
- Export remains local until the user explicitly chooses an operating-system
  destination.
- Representative short and long histories render without clipping.
